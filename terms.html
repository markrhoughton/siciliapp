8. About the content

Siciliapp teaches Sicilian as spoken around Messina. I have worked hard to make the cards accurate, but please keep in mind:

  • Sicilian varies a lot from town to town and across generations. A word or spelling on a card may be different where your family comes from — that does not necessarily make either one wrong.
  • Sicilian has no single official spelling. The app uses a consistent, readable spelling that may differ from other sources.
  • The cards are educational material, not a certified or professional translation. Please do not rely on them for legal, medical, official or other important communication.

If you spot a mistake, please tell me. I read every message.
